ABOUT ME

I like figuring out how all the pieces fit together.

I’m a Computer Engineering graduate working toward my MBA in Project Management. What I’ve learned about myself is that I really enjoy the part before the finished product — understanding the problem, figuring out what people need, and deciding how everything should work together.

That is the mindset I brought into AssetTrack, Blueprint, and StudyFlow. They are different products, but each one started with a real problem that I wanted to make easier. I planned the workflows, made decisions about how the systems should work, built and refined them, tested them, and thought about what I would improve next.

I’m building toward technical program and project leadership because I want to understand the technology while also helping connect the technical work to the bigger business goal. I like structure, communication, problem solving, and making sure good ideas actually move forward.

Blueprint — How I approached it

Product thinking, AI architecture, roadmap

›

Problem

Job seekers can find openings but still struggle to understand which jobs fit them, what skills they are missing, and how to become stronger candidates.

Why It Mattered

Blueprint came from something I experienced personally while moving from college into the job market.

Approach

I defined the core user questions, planned the product foundation, organized the roadmap, and built the AI flow through a secure backend.

Solution

A full-stack foundation for career recommendations, skill-gap analysis, resume improvement, job discovery, and personalized guidance.

Decisions

I focused on scalability, security, maintainability, reusable components, and keeping sensitive AI credentials server-side.

Next

StudyFlow — How I approached it

Workflow consolidation, modular design, AI assistance

›

Problem

Students often manage courses, assignments, calendars, study tools, and AI across several disconnected applications.

Approach

I organized those disconnected activities into one student workflow covering courses, assignments, exams, study planning, focus, and analytics.

Solution

StudyFlow combines course management, assignment tracking, a study planner, exam center, Pomodoro timer, analytics, backup/restore, and AI assistance.

Architecture

Modular React components and shared state connect to an Express backend that handles telemetry and Google Gemini requests.

What I Learned

Full-stack development, modular architecture, API integration, user workflow design, and building around a practical productivity problem.

Next

Canvas integration, Google Calendar sync, authentication, cloud persistence, multi-device support, and mobile.
