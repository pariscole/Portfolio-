›

Problem

Hardware information can become difficult to manage when inventory, employee assignments, maintenance, warranties, and financial information are tracked separately.

My Role

I defined business requirements and workflows, planned the application structure and user experience, organized realistic sample data, and managed version control with Git and GitHub.

Development

I used Google AI Studio as part of my AI-assisted development process, reviewed and refined generated code, and developed workflows connecting the major areas of the application.

Testing

I tested application functionality, debugged issues, and checked workflows across inventory, employees, maintenance, warranties, reporting, and other modules.

Features

Hardware inventory, employee assignments, maintenance tickets, warranty tracking, QR labels, depreciation information, CSV import/export, dashboards, and analytics.

Next

Authentication, cloud database support, role-based access, audit logs, notifications, REST APIs, and mobile support.

SheLeads Innovation Hub — Product Strategy Capstone

Product strategy, PRD, research, prioritization, product policy

›

Project

I developed product strategy for a proposed AI-powered mentorship and career platform as part of my Fall 2025 product management capstone through the Black Venture Capital Consortium.

User Need

The product concept focused on mentorship, sponsorship, career visibility, professional development, and access to opportunities for Black women professionals.

Research & Analysis

I worked through personas, jobs-to-be-done, market and competitive analysis, and SWOT analysis to identify user needs and opportunities.

PRD

I created a brief product requirements document that defined the product problem, user value, a user story, proposed success metrics, and product policy considerations.

Prioritization

I used the MoSCoW framework to organize proposed features by priority and connect product decisions back to the core user problem.

Product Policy

The product policy considered human oversight, transparency, fairness, and privacy as part of the proposed AI-powered experience.
